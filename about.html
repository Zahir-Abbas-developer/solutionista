The Founder

The Strategist Behind the Stillness

Destani Sabree built her career as an operational strategist, the person leaders trusted to see the whole board. Then she stepped out of the rat race to build something of her own: a practice where that bird’s-eye operational design meets intentional, hands-on care.

Today, The Solutionista holds both worlds. In the treatment room, she refines, sculpts, and restores. In your operations, she removes friction and architects calm. On stage, she carries the room. Different arenas, one standard.

“Every service I offer answers the same question: how do I return your time, your energy, and your ease?”
